# GRC Portfolio

Hi, I'm Dhriti Pandya, a cybersecurity student at the University of South Carolina Aiken with a growing focus on Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC).

I created this portfolio to document the practical GRC skills I am developing through hands-on projects. My work focuses on risk assessment, security controls, governance, policy development, and applying cybersecurity frameworks to realistic business scenarios.

## Projects

### [Project 1 - Enterprise Cybersecurity Risk Assessment](01-Enterprise-Cybersecurity-Risk-Assessment)

For this project, I conducted a cybersecurity risk assessment for BlueWave Retail Solutions, a fictional retail and e-commerce organization.

I identified assets, threats, and vulnerabilities; developed a risk register using a 5x5 likelihood and impact methodology; evaluated inherent and residual risk; proposed security controls and risk treatments; and connected the assessment to the NIST Cybersecurity Framework.

**Skills demonstrated:** Cybersecurity Risk Assessment, Risk Registers, Risk Analysis, Security Controls, Risk Treatment, NIST CSF

---

### [Project 2 - AI Governance & Risk Management Program](02-AI-Governance-Risk-Management)

For this project, I developed an AI Governance and Risk Management Program for NovaTech Solutions, a fictional organization using generative AI across multiple departments.

I assessed six AI risks involving sensitive data, inaccurate AI output, shadow AI, compliance, intellectual property, and AI-generated code. I then developed proposed controls and risk treatments, created an AI Governance & Acceptable Use Policy, and connected the program to the NIST AI RMF and NIST CSF 2.0.

**Skills demonstrated:** AI Governance, AI Risk Assessment, Risk Treatment, Security Controls, Policy Development, NIST AI RMF, NIST CSF 2.0

---

## Frameworks Used

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)

## About This Portfolio

These projects use fictional organizations and scenarios for educational and portfolio purposes. Controls and recommendations are proposed based on the scenarios rather than implemented or tested in production environments.

I will continue adding projects as I build my skills in cybersecurity GRC, control assessment, compliance, IT audit, and third-party risk management.
