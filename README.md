# GRC-Portfolio
Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) cybersecurity portfolio featuring hands-on risk assessment, security controls, risk treatment, and cybersecurity framework projects.

## Projects

### [Project 1 — Enterprise Cybersecurity Risk Assessment](01-Enterprise-Cybersecurity-Risk-Assessment)
Conducted a simulated enterprise cybersecurity risk assessment for BlueWave Retail Solutions, a fictional mid-sized retail and e-commerce organization.

The project includes asset identification, threat and vulnerability analysis, a 5×5 likelihood and impact risk methodology, an enterprise risk register, inherent and residual risk analysis, security control recommendations, risk treatment, and NIST Cybersecurity Framework mapping.

Skills demonstrated: Cybersecurity Risk Assessment, GRC, Risk Register Development, Risk Analysis, Security Controls, Risk Treatment, and NIST CSF.
