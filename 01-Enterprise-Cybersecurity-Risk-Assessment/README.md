# Enterprise Cybersecurity Risk Assessment
## Project Overview

This project demonstrates an enterprise cybersecurity risk assessment conducted for BlueWave Retail Solutions, a fictional mid-sized retail and e-commerce organization.

The purpose of this project was to identify cybersecurity risks affecting critical business and technology assets, evaluate those risks using a structured risk assessment methodology, recommend appropriate security controls, and develop risk treatment strategies.

## Assessment Scope

The assessment covers ten key business and technology assets:

- Customer Database
- Microsoft 365
- Active Directory
- Employee Laptops
- POS Systems
- Corporate Wi-Fi
- Public Website
- Backup Systems
- HR Information System
- Financial Management System

## Risk Assessment Methodology

Cybersecurity risks were evaluated using a 5×5 Likelihood and Impact methodology.

Likelihood and impact were each rated on a scale from 1 to 5.

Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

Risk scores were classified as:

- Low: 1–4
- Medium: 5–9
- High: 10–16
- Critical: 17–25

The assessment also evaluates estimated residual risk after recommended security controls are implemented.

## Key Findings

The assessment identified risks involving unauthorized access, phishing, credential compromise, device theft, malware, unauthorized wireless access, DDoS attacks, ransomware, inappropriate access to sensitive HR information, and financial fraud.

The highest inherent risk identified involved ransomware affecting backup systems, with an inherent risk score of 20 (Critical).

Recommended controls include multi-factor authentication, role-based access control, encryption, patch management, network segmentation, security awareness training, audit logging, segregation of duties, DDoS protection, and offline or immutable backups.

## NIST Cybersecurity Framework

The assessment maps cybersecurity activities to the five NIST Cybersecurity Framework functions:

- Identify: Asset inventory, assessment scope, threat and vulnerability identification, and risk assessment.
- Protect: MFA, RBAC, encryption, patch management, network segmentation, security awareness training, and access controls.
- Detect: Audit logging, security monitoring, availability monitoring, and periodic access reviews.
- Respond: Incident response procedures, containment, escalation, and stakeholder communication.
- Recover: Offline or immutable backups, restoration testing, recovery procedures, and restoration of critical business operations.
